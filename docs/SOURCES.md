## Known caveats

EVA `ch`, `sh`, `ckh`, `cth`, `cph`, `cfh` are heuristically grouped in the computational experiments. Transcriptions share a manuscript and metadata, and are not independent samples of authored text. Differences may reflect spacing, line splitting, or character recognition; align before claiming a glyph disagreement. Images must be reviewed to determine which reading, if either, is right.
